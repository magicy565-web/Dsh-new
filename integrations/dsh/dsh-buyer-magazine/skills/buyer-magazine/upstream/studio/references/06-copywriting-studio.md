# 06 · Copywriting studio

## Contract

**Input:** editorial plan and admitted facts. **Output:** concise English buyer copy in the issue fields, with optional deliberately authored localization. **Consumer:** renderer, email digest, print and exported inquiry. Write every line so it remains truthful when viewed out of context.

## Write the headline from the decision

Use a product/category plus a concrete angle: “Compact lighting, with trade terms still to confirm”. Avoid unsupported superlatives, “revolutionary”, “best factory”, “guaranteed margins”, and unmeasured market-growth claims. Make a deck add information, not restate the headline.

Prefer short product titles and descriptive category labels. Keep headline and deck lengths as design targets, not hard truncation rules. Rewrite for fit; never remove a qualifying word just to prevent a third line.

## Use the fields for distinct jobs

| Field | Job | Typical target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cover.headline | Establish the issue's decision or angle | 5–12 words |
| cover.dek | Explain scope and utility | 25–45 words |
| product.summary | Describe the actual product and sourced differentiator | 35–65 words |
| product.buyerFit | State an editorial interpretation for the buyer | 20–40 words |
| product.watchout | Surface the most material uncertainty | 15–35 words |
| specification label | Name the property without ambiguity | 1–5 words |
| CTA label | Describe the actual next action | 2–5 words |
| image alt | Explain visually relevant content without repeating every caption | As short as useful |

Do not force every field to its maximum. Avoid repeating a numeric specification in summary, a callout, and the table unless each occurrence serves a clear scanning purpose.

## Distinguish claim, inference and unknown

| Weak or misleading | Publishable rewrite |
| --- | --- |
| Factory-direct price $39 | Listed retail price: USD 39 |
| Ideal for every US retailer | May suit a compact home assortment; assess market fit |
| MOQ available on request | MOQ not disclosed in the reviewed source |
| Certified for Europe | Supplier states compliance; supporting document not reviewed |
| Fast delivery | Keep the exact sourced lead time and what it covers |
| Premium sustainable finish | Describe the sourced material/finish; omit sustainability without evidence |
| Verified manufacturer | Supplier identity and manufacturing role need separate verification |

Use “not disclosed” for an absent source fact. Use “not independently verified” for a known assertion lacking independent evidence. Use “request a quote” when it is a legitimate next action, not a substitute for disclosing missing information.

## Localize with commercial precision

Keep the default reader copy English for overseas buyers. Use plain international English and ISO currency codes when currency symbols could be ambiguous. Preserve model numbers, units, certification identifiers and quoted terms exactly. Do not translate Incoterms into vague shipping claims. Express dates unambiguously, for example “26 Oct 2026”.

For a Chinese edition, author the copy intentionally and check line breaks with a CJK font stack. Do not imply that setting `locale: "zh-CN"` translates existing English fields or every fixed interface label automatically. The current renderer's supported language behavior must be inspected.

## Write inquiry hooks as specific questions

Good: “Ask for the private-label MOQ, carton dimensions, and production lead time for the 220 V model.” Weak: “Contact us for the best price.” The first supports a business decision; the second adds friction and makes a claim.

Keep the email subject and preheader useful if an email provider is added later. The current package exports email content; do not describe that export as a sent newsletter.

## Gate

Search the draft for certainty terms: certified, verified, factory, guaranteed, compliant, exclusive, fastest, cheapest, sustainable, wholesale. Check each instance against its evidence. Inspect titles, alt text, captions and exports as well as body copy. A careful footnote does not repair a misleading headline.
