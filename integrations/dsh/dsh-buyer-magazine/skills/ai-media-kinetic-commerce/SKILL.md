## Preserved original design method

# AI Media & Kinetic Commerce — 5-Spread Interactive Magazine Corporate Card Orchestrator

Transforms any target corporate or brand URL into a gallery-grade **5-Spread Interactive Magazine Corporate Digital Card** designed for B2B/DTC client acquisition and high-trust conversion.

## Core Architecture & Orchestration Workflow

1. **Step 1: Real Corporate DNA Extraction (`apify-ecommerce-intelligence`)**
   - Extract real brand positioning, B2B/DTC authority metrics, core product/service pillars, and authentic CDN image assets from the input URL.
2. **Step 2: Magazine Archetype & Materiality Routing (`editorial-magazine-design`)**
   - Route the brand to Archetype A (Kinfolk Slow-Living), B (Loewe Monograph), C (Monocle Broadsheet), or D (032c Technical Archive).
   - Configure paper grain overlays, 3D book spine gutters, and dual-mode navigation (**Spread Flip Mode** + **Editorial Scroll Mode**).
3. **Step 3: Multi-Voice Typographic Choreography (`editorial-typography-system`)**
   - Apply staircase multi-voice headlines, CJK-Latin optical calibration, OpenType tabular numerals, and continuous pilcrow prose.
4. **Step 4: Live Visual Relay & VIP-Gated Video Teaser**
   - **Image Generation Relay**: Use OpenAI-compatible `https://www.fastaitoken.com/v1` endpoint for missing or art-directed editorial plates.
   - **Strict VIP-Gated Video Upsell**: Never invoke live video generation directly on load; present an interactive filmstrip / darkroom teaser card that requires lead verification or VIP unlock to request custom motion lookbooks.
5. **Step 5: Interactive Conversion Apparatus**
   - **Spread 03 Centerfold**: Interactive Industry Sandbox & B2B/C2M ROI / Unit-Economics Calculator.
   - **Spread 04 Back Cover**: Tear-Out Requisition Postcard with 3D Cinnabar Wax Seal animation + Instant `.vcf` (vCard) Executive Contact Export.
