# 首封开发信模板

主题行（≤ 6 词，选一个，替换 `<...>`）：

- `<Product> supplier for <Company>`
- `<Company>'s <category> line — sample available`
- `New <material/feature> <product> for <market>`
- `Re: <Company> <category> range`

正文（≤ 120 词）：

```
Hi <First name>,

I saw <Company> carries <specific product/category> for <market>. We make
<product> for <buyer type> in <country>, and <one concrete proof: certification,
capacity, lead time, or a named market you already ship to>.

Two things that may matter for your line:
- <benefit with a number: e.g. 25-day lead time, MOQ 300 pcs, CE + RoHS on file>
- <second benefit: custom packaging, mixed containers, exclusive territory>

Would a sample be useful? I can send one <this week / with your logo> so your
team can check the finish.

<Name>, <Title>, <Company>
<website> · <WhatsApp> · <city, country>
```

## 发送前检查

- 称呼是具体的人，不是 `Dear Sir/Madam`。
- 第一句只讲对方，不讲自己。
- 没有附件，没有图片，没有超过 5 个链接。
- 只有一个问题，答案是「是 / 否」。
- 退订或「不感兴趣请忽略」一句都没有也可以；不要写「如不需要请回复退订」。

## 不要做

- 群发 BCC、同一封模板发给 50 家。
- 首封就发价格表或 10MB 目录。
- 用 `Best price`、`High quality`、`We are a leading manufacturer` 开场。
- 承诺做不到的交期或认证。
